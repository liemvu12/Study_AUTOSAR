# Official YouTube Policy & Regulation Index

This reference document compiles canonical URLs, official definitions, and enforcement criteria directly from official Google Support and YouTube documentation.

---

## 1. YouTube Partner Program (YPP) Overview & Eligibility

- **Canonical URL:** [YouTube Partner Program overview & eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)
- **Minimum Eligibility Thresholds (Full Ad Revenue Share):**
  1. Follow YouTube channel monetization policies.
  2. Live in a country/region where YPP is available.
  3. Have no active Community Guidelines strikes on your channel.
  4. Have 2-Step Verification turned on for your Google Account.
  5. Have advanced features access on YouTube.
  6. Have one active AdSense account linked to your channel.
  7. **Metrics Threshold (Either/Or):**
     - Gain **1,000 subscribers** with **4,000 valid public watch hours** in the last 12 months, OR
     - Gain **1,000 subscribers** with **10 million valid public Shorts views** in the last 90 days.
- **Early Access Threshold (Fan Funding & Shopping only):**
  - **500 subscribers** + **3 valid public uploads** in the last 90 days + **3,000 valid public watch hours** in the last 12 months (or **3 million Shorts views** in the last 90 days).

## 2. YouTube Channel Monetization Policies: Reused & Repetitious Content

- **Canonical URL:** [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- **Enforcement Body:** Human reviewers evaluate channels against these guidelines before approval and during periodic re-reviews.

### A. Reused Content (Nội dung sử dụng lại)
- **Official Definition:** Content that doesn't provide significant original commentary or educational value. It also means that we’ve evaluated your channel and found that the spirit of the policy wasn’t followed.
- **What is NOT allowed to monetize:**
  - Clips of other people's content edited together with minimal to no narrative changes.
  - Short videos you compiled from other social media websites without adding value.
  - Collections of songs from different artists (even if you have their permission).
  - Content uploaded many times by other creators.
  - Promotion of other people’s content (even if you have permission).
- **What IS allowed to monetize:**
  - A funny or thoughtful revision of content you didn't originally create.
  - Clips of other people's material as part of a critical review or commentary.
  - A scene from a movie where you’ve re-written the dialogue and changed the voiceover.
  - Replays of a sports match where you explain the moves a competitor made to succeed (or fail).
  - Reaction videos where you comment on the original video.
  - Edited footage from other creators where you add a unique narrative or commentary.

### B. Repetitious Content (Nội dung lặp đi lặp lại)
- **Official Definition:** Content that appears mass-produced or generated by a program without adding significant original value, or content where viewers cannot easily tell the difference between videos on the channel.
- **What is NOT allowed to monetize:**
  - Content that is exclusively generated by software or templates with minimal human editorial input.
  - Content with synthetic voice narration reading third-party text or websites with no added insight.
  - Similar or identical content uploaded repeatedly across multiple videos with slight parameter variations.
  - Programmatically generated clips that flood the platform without distinct creative identity.

## 3. Policy on Altered or Synthetic Content (AI Disclosure)

- **Canonical URL:** [How to disclose altered or synthetic content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)
- **Mandatory Disclosure Requirement:**
  - Creators must disclose when content is altered or synthetic (including generative AI) if it **appears realistic** to an ordinary viewer.
  - *What must be disclosed:*
    - Making a real person appear to say or do something they didn't say or do.
    - Altering footage of a real event or place.
    - Generating a realistic-looking scene that didn't actually occur (e.g., a fake natural disaster).
  - *What does NOT require disclosure:*
    - Clearly unrealistic content (animation, fantasy creatures, sci-fi backgrounds).
    - Beauty filters, color grading, visual effects (VFX).
    - AI used for production assistance (generating video outlines, scripts, brainstorms, or generating automated captions).
- **Enforcement & Consequences:**
  - Failure to disclose may result in YouTube adding a permanent label to the video, video removal, or suspension from the YouTube Partner Program.

## 4. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) & "Made for Kids"

- **Canonical URL:** [Determining if your content is "Made for Kids"](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076)
- **Legal Context:** Agreement with the US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) to comply with COPPA.
- **Key Indicators of "Made for Kids":**
  - Children are the primary target audience.
  - Child actors, characters, toys, songs, stories, or activities appealing specifically to children.
- **Monetization Impact when marked "Made for Kids":**
  - Personalized ads are disabled by law. Only contextual ads can run (resulting in significant RPM drop).
  - Comments are permanently disabled.
  - Channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, and end screens are disabled.
  - Video will not appear in the Miniplayer.

## 5. Advertiser-Friendly Content Guidelines

- **Canonical URL:** [Advertiser-friendly content guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278)
- **Monetization Impact:** Determines whether a video gets a green icon (monetized), yellow icon (limited or no ads), or red icon (not monetized).
- **Core Sensitive Categories:**
  - Inappropriate language (profanity within the first 7 seconds vs. later in video).
  - Violence and graphic content.
  - Adult content and sexually suggestive themes.
  - Shocking content.
  - Harmful or dangerous acts.
  - Hateful & derogatory content.
  - Firearms and weapons.
  - Sensitive events (armed conflicts, natural disasters, mass tragedies).

## 6. YouTube Terms of Service: Automated Access & Scraping

- **Canonical URL:** [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms)
- **Section 5.3 (Permissions and Restrictions):**
  - *"You are not allowed to: access the Service using any automated means (such as robots, botnets or scrapers) except (a) in the case of public search engines... or (b) with YouTube’s prior written permission."*
- **Compliance Requirement for Agents & Skills:**
  - Never recommend scraping YouTube HTML or using headless scrapers that violate YouTube TOS.
  - Always mandate the official **YouTube Data API v3** (`developers.google.com/youtube/v3`) for retrieving platform metrics within established API quotas.
